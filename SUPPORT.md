# Support — StealthBridge Contracts

> **Stellar Testnet engineering only.** StealthBridge does not operate Mainnet custody, fiat payouts, or live commercial remittances. Never send real XLM or Mainnet assets to Testnet addresses.

## Where to ask questions

- **Soroban contracts, WASM builds, and Testnet evidence:** Search [stealthbridge-contracts issues](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/stealthbridge-contracts/issues) and read [docs/DEPLOYMENT.md](deployments/testnet/OPERATOR-DEPLOYMENT-RUNBOOK.md) or [MILESTONE-6-RELEASE-READINESS-REPORT.md](docs/MILESTONE-6-RELEASE-READINESS-REPORT.md).
- **Privacy rails & ZK evaluation:** See [MILESTONE-4-PRIVACY-RAILS-EVALUATION.md](docs/MILESTONE-4-PRIVACY-RAILS-EVALUATION.md) and [tools/privacy-rail-poc/README.md](tools/privacy-rail-poc/README.md).
- **Cross-repository integration:** Check the [organization profile](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md) and [CROSS-REPO-INTEGRATION-MAP.md](docs/CROSS-REPO-INTEGRATION-MAP.md).

## Before opening an issue

Include commit SHA, `rustc --version`, `stellar --version`, and exact failing test or build output. **Never paste private keys, mnemonics, or ZK witnesses.**

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) privately.
